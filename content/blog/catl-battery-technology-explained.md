The **Chery Wanda** is a premier commercial **EV microbus** powered by a cutting-edge CATL battery pack using advanced lithium iron phosphate (LFP) chemistry. It features a 41.86 kWh capacity on the 11 and 12-seater variants, and a robust 53.58 kWh pack on the 14 and 16-seater models. Available through **Trijal Motors**, the authorized dealer, CATL cells are engineered by the world's largest battery manufacturer—powering global giants like Tesla, BMW, Toyota, and Volkswagen. Here is what this world-class **EV** technology means for local route operators.

## LFP vs NMC Chemistry for Commercial EVs

Most early electric vehicle models used nickel-manganese-cobalt (NMC) chemistry, which offers high energy density but suffers from faster degradation under deep daily charge cycles. The CATL LFP cells built into the **Chery Wanda EV microbus** are inherently more stable. They can be safely charged to 100% daily without accelerating battery degradation, giving transport operators maximum daily range security.

## Temperature Performance Across Nepal and Gandaki Province

Nepal's topography demands an **EV** that handles extreme climate ranges—from below-freezing conditions on high-altitude routes to 40°C+ heat in the Terai plains. LFP battery chemistry performs exceptionally well across this spectrum. Commercial fleet operators running the vital **EV Pokhara** to Jomsom corridor report no significant range reduction during winter conditions.

## Extended Cycle Life for Maximum Profitability

CATL LFP cells are rated for over 3,000+ charge cycles while maintaining less than 20% capacity loss. At one full charge per day, that translates to more than 8 years of dependable service before any meaningful degradation occurs. In practice, many **Trijal Motors** clients charge strategically to 80% daily, extending this commercial lifespan even further.

## Efficient Fast Charging Capabilities

To keep your fleet running smoothly, the **Chery Wanda** supports both AC overnight charging (8–10 hours from empty) and powerful DC fast charging (reaching 80% in approximately 40 minutes). For busy routes around the **EV Pokhara** network, we recommend utilizing DC fast charging for quick mid-day top-ups and standard AC charging overnight.

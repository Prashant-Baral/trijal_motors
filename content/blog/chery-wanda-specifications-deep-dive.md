## Battery Architecture: The CATL Advantage

At the core of every Chery Wanda EV is a battery manufactured by CATL, the global leader in electric vehicle energy storage.

| Feature | Specification Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Battery Chemistry** | Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) |
| **Capacity Options** | 41.86 kWh and 53.58 kWh |
| **Rated Power** | Up to 80 kW |
| **Charging Protocol** | Standard GBT Fast Charging |

Unlike older EV chemistries, Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) cells are highly celebrated for their exceptional thermal stability. This means the battery maintains peak performance and experiences minimal degradation even when working hard against the steep climbs out of the Pokhara valley during hot summer months.

## Variant Breakdown: Matching Range to Payload Capacity

The Chery Wanda line is split into four distinct configurations, allowing fleet managers to select the precise balance of passenger capacity, roof height, and range:

### 11-Seater & 12-Seater (Low Roof Variants)
*   **Battery Capacity:** 41.86 kWh.
*   **Range (NEDC):** Approximately 280 km to 300 km on a single charge.
*   **Best Used For:** These agile, low-roof models are highly optimized for localized urban travel, corporate staff commutes, and short school routes within the Pokhara metropolitan area.

### 14-Seater & 16-Seater (High Roof Variants)
*   **Battery Capacity:** 53.58 kWh, delivering up to 80 kW of rated power.
*   **Best Used For:** Built with an elevated high roof for maximum passenger comfort, these variants are designed for long-distance highway travel, intercity commuting, and tourism transportation between major hubs.

## Terrain Adaptability and Ground Clearance

Navigating rural detours and seasonal highway maintenance requires excellent physical clearance. The Chery Wanda features a robust ground clearance range spanning from 180mm up to 210mm (depending on the variant and load conditions).

Combined with a sophisticated suspension system—featuring a double-wishbone torsion bar setup—the vehicle actively dampens structural vibrations on gravelly patches, keeping passengers comfortable and protecting the under-vehicle battery enclosure from debris.

## Maximizing Your "Mileage": Best Practices for Drivers

To help your team get the absolute highest number of kilometers out of every single charge, teach your drivers to adopt three essential driving habits:

1.  **Anticipate Traffic Smoothly:** Avoid sudden, aggressive acceleration. Accelerating smoothly keeps energy draw stable and extends your battery life.
2.  **Optimize Climate Control Usage:** Running the cabin air conditioning at maximum power on hot summer afternoons increases your energy consumption. Pre-cooling the cabin while the vehicle is still plugged into your overnight charger saves valuable battery power for the road.
3.  **Manage Fast-Charging Wisely:** For highway long-distance runs, it is most efficient to unplug once the battery reaches an 80% State of Charge (SoC). The vehicle's charging speed naturally slows down after 80% to protect the battery cells. Unplugging at 80% saves time and keeps your trip moving quickly.

## The Regulatory Advantage: Understanding Loan-to-Value (LTV) Limits

The biggest financial advantage of buying a commercial electric vehicle in Nepal lies in the generous **Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratios** set by the central bank.

When purchasing a traditional petrol or diesel passenger vehicle, buyers often face strict financing caps that require substantial upfront down payments. Because the government is actively pushing to modernize public and commercial transport, electric alternatives enjoy highly supportive terms:

*   **Favorable 40/60 Financing Structure:** Under current credit guidelines, commercial banks are authorized to finance **up to 60%** of the total value of an electric commercial vehicle.
*   **Accessible Down Payment Entry Point:** The buyer is only required to cover a **minimum 40% down payment** upfront.

This highly accessible framework allows businesses to preserve their valuable working capital. Instead of tying up large sums of cash in an expensive upfront vehicle purchase, companies can deploy their capital into expanding their operations while letting the vehicle's daily fuel savings pay off the loan balance.

## Corporate Underwriting: Your Documentation Checklist

When applying for a commercial vehicle loan at a bank in Pokhara, speed is everything. The credit department requires a complete, well-organized application file to move forward with an appraisal. Gathering these essential items early ensures a fast, hassle-free approval process:

### 1. Corporate Identity and Registration Proof
*   Official Company Registration Certificate from the Office of the Company Registrar.
*   Valid PAN or VAT Registration Certificates.
*   Up-to-date tax clearance documents for the previous fiscal year.
*   The firm's Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA) if operating as a private limited company.

### 2. Financial Standing and Cash Flow Validation
*   Audited financial statements (Balance Sheets and Income Statements) covering the past 2 to 3 fiscal years.
*   Certified bank account statements for the last 6 months to demonstrate stable, active cash flows.
*   A formal Board Resolution signed by the company promoters authorizing the vehicle purchase and nominating individuals to manage the bank loan.

### 3. Vehicle Verification Records
*   An official, customized corporate quotation issued directly by Trijal Motors detailing your selected Chery Wanda variant.
*   Technical specification sheets validating the vehicle's battery configuration and commercial safety features. To review these structural and electrical specifications in detail, check out our [Chery Wanda specifications deep dive](/blog/chery-wanda-specifications-deep-dive).

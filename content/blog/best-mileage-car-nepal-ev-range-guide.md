When people search for the best mileage, they are trying to minimize their daily cost per kilometer. Traditional petrol hatchbacks look efficient on paper, but steep mountain climbs, heavy traffic, and idling dramatically lower their real-world numbers.

- Advertised Efficiency: 18 km/Liter (Ideal Highway conditions)
- Real-World Local Traffic Efficiency: ~11 to 12 km/Liter
- **Running Cost per Kilometer: ~Rs. 14 to Rs. 16 per km**

### Modern Electric Vehicle Platform

- Standard Energy Consumption: 1 Unit (kWh) of electricity gives ~7 Kilometers
- **Running Cost per Kilometer: ~Rs. 1.4 to Rs. 1.8 per km**

## Advanced Regeneration: Making Energy From Uphill Climbs

The most unique feature of an electric vehicle's mileage profile is its ability to **generate energy while driving downhill**.

When descending a winding route like the highway from Kande to Pokhara, traditional petrol cars constantly wear down their brakes and waste energy as heat. An electric vehicle uses its **Regenerative Braking System** to convert that downhill momentum back into electricity, actively recharging the battery and increasing your driving range without costing a single rupee.

## Understanding the GBT Protocol: The Golden Standard in Nepal

When evaluating electric transport options in Nepal, charging plug compatibility is incredibly important. The Chery Wanda platform utilizes the standard **GBT (Guobiao/T) charging protocol** for both alternating current (AC) slow charging and direct current (DC) fast charging.

Why does this matter for your business? The Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) along with major private charging networks have established GBT as the standard infrastructure setup for heavy commercial vehicles, buses, and microbuses across the country's national highways.

Investing in a GBT-compliant fleet means your drivers can pull into any public fast-charging hub along the Prithvi Highway, the Siddhartha Highway, or the BP Highway and plug in immediately without needing expensive, unreliable adapters.

## Charging Metrics: Speed vs. Power Source

The time it takes to charge a Chery Wanda microbus depends directly on the variant's battery capacity (41.86 kWh for low-roof models vs. 53.58 kWh for high-roof models) and the output capacity of the charger you choose.

### 1. Dedicated AC Slow Charging (Overnight Fleet Hubs)
*   **Power Output:** Typically 3.3 kW to 7 kW or 11 kW AC.
*   **Time to Full Charge:** Approximately 6.5 to 11 hours.
*   **Best Used For:** Schools, corporate offices, and local resorts can set up simple wall-mounted AC chargers at their main garages. After completing daytime routes, vehicles plug in overnight, drawing lower-tariff off-peak electricity to reach 100% capacity by morning.

### 2. High-Output DC Fast Charging (Highway Corridors)
*   **Power Output:** 30 kW to 60 kW and above.
*   **Time to Charge (30% to 80%):** Under 45 to 60 minutes.
*   **Best Used For:** Intercity tourism operators driving between Pokhara, Kathmandu, and Chitwan. During a lunch break or a passenger rest stop at Malekhu, Mugling, or Kurintar, the driver plugs into a public DC fast charger, adding over 150 km of driving range in the time it takes passengers to finish a meal.

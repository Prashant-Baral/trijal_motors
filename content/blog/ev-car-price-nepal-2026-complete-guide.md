## The EV Price Architecture in Nepal

The electric car market is broadly organized into three main price segments, making it easy to see what your budget will buy:

### Entry-Level Budget Segment — NPR 25 Lakhs to 35 Lakhs

- **Ideal For:** Daily city commuting, easy parking, and maximum urban efficiency.
- **Expected Range:** 300 km to 320 km (CLTC/Urban Cycles).
- **Core Focus:** Highly practical technology, compact footprints, and low initial capital outlay.

### Mid-Range Value Segment — NPR 35 Lakhs to 55 Lakhs

- **Ideal For:** Balanced family use, weekend highway trips, and extra cargo space.
- **Expected Range:** 400 km to 470 km on a single charge.
- **Core Focus:** Advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), spacious cabins, and enhanced power.

### Premium Luxury Segment — NPR 55 Lakhs to 75 Lakhs+

- **Ideal For:** Long-distance touring, executive transport, and top-tier luxury performance.
- **Expected Range:** Up to 500+ km with large, high-capacity battery packs.
- **Core Focus:** High-performance all-wheel-drive (AWD) systems and premium interior materials.

## The True Cost Beyond the Showroom Sticker Price

When comparing traditional petrol vehicles to modern electric options, looking only at the initial showroom price sheet doesn't tell the whole story. Traditional internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicles carry massive long-term liabilities: high customs duties up to 238%, rising fuel prices, and expensive routine engine servicing.

Electric vehicles benefit from supportive government policies, including significantly lower import taxes, which directly brings down the showroom price compared to gas-powered equivalents with similar features.

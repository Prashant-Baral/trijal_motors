## The Core Math: Fuel vs. Kilowatt-Hours

Let's look at the daily operating costs for a standard passenger vehicle traveling an average of **50 kilometers per day (1,500 km per month)** on local routes:

### Traditional Petrol Passenger Vehicle

- Average Fuel Efficiency: ~12 Kilometers per Liter
- Current Fuel Cost: ~Rs. 175 per Liter
- Daily Fuel Cost (50 km): Rs. 730
- **Total Monthly Fuel Overhead: Rs. 21,900**

### Modern Electric Passenger Vehicle

- Average Energy Consumption: ~1 Unit (kWh) per 7 Kilometers
- Average Domestic Electricity Tariff: ~Rs. 10 per Unit
- Daily Charging Cost (50 km): ~Rs. 71
- **Total Monthly Electricity Overhead: Rs. 2,130**

### Net Monthly Cash Savings: Rs. 19,770

By switching to domestic electricity, you cut your monthly energy expenses down by **nearly 90%**, keeping almost Rs. 20,000 in your pocket every single month. ## The Hidden Savings: Zero Engine Maintenance

The financial benefits of moving to an electric platform go far beyond what you save on fuel. Traditional internal combustion engines contain thousands of moving parts that require regular, expensive maintenance:

1.  **No Engine Oil Changes:** Say goodbye to regular garage visits for expensive engine oil, oil filters, air filters, and spark plug replacements.
2.  **No Complex Timing Belts:** Electric vehicles don't use complex multi-speed transmissions, clutches, or timing belts, completely eliminating some of the most expensive repair risks that petrol car owners face.
3.  **Longer Brake Life:** Advanced **Regenerative Braking Systems** use the electric motor to slow the vehicle down, capturing energy to recharge the battery while significantly reducing wear on your brake pads and discs.

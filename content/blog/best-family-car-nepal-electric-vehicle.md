## Space Redefined: The Electric Platform Advantage

Electric vehicles are built on specialized, flat chassis layouts that create significantly more interior cabin space than traditional petrol cars:

*   **No Transmission Tunnel:** Because there is no bulky exhaust system or mechanical transmission tunnel running through the middle of the floor, the rear seat floor is completely flat, giving your kids plenty of comfortable legroom.
*   **Maximum Headroom and Luggage Space:** Moving mechanical components out of the way allows designers to create larger cabins and generous trunk space, making it easy to pack strollers, groceries, and travel bags for family trips.

## Structural Engineering: The Full-Load Monocoque Advantage

At the core of the Chery Wanda's safety architecture is a design shift away from standard ladder-frame chassis configurations. The Chery Wanda uses a **high-strength steel monocoque body structure**, engineered specifically for right-hand drive markets.

### Integrated Impact Zones
In a monocoque design, the body and chassis are integrated into a single high-rigidity structure. The cabin is surrounded by a high-strength steel safety cage with dedicated crumple zones at the front and rear. In the event of a collision, impact energy is directed away from the passenger cabin, shielding occupants from severe injury.

### Lower Center of Gravity
Unlike traditional diesel microbuses that place a heavy engine high up in the front bay, the Chery Wanda houses its flat CATL battery packs securely underneath the floor frame. This lower weight distribution significantly reduces the vehicle's center of gravity. The result is excellent stability on winding mountain corners, a greatly reduced risk of body roll, and a stable, confident ride even when carrying a full payload of passengers and luggage.

## High-Voltage Electrical Safety: IP67 Waterproofing and Beyond

A common concern among fleet operators in Pokhara involves driving an electric vehicle through heavy monsoon rains, deep puddles, or waterlogged rural crossings. To handle these exact conditions, Chery Wanda's high-voltage electrical architecture features layered safety engineering:

*   **IP67 Ingress Protection:** The entire high-voltage battery enclosure, motor assembly, and electronic control units carry an IP67 rating. This means the critical electrical systems are completely sealed against dust and can withstand temporary immersion in water without any electrical leakage.
*   **Battery Shields and Guard Plates:** To protect the battery packs from rocks, flying debris, and rough under-car scrapes on unpaved resort access roads, the under-chassis is fitted with heavy-duty metal guard plates. This protection keeps the battery housing safe from puncture or structural distortion.
*   **Automatic High-Voltage Disconnect:** The vehicle is equipped with a smart crash sensor network. In the event of a significant impact or sudden airbag deployment, the onboard system instantly cuts off high-voltage power from the battery pack in milliseconds, completely eliminating any risk of electrical shock to passengers or emergency responders.

## Active Safety Mechanics: ABS, EBD, and Driver Assistance

Preventing an accident from happening is always the best safety outcome. The Chery Wanda integrates essential active safety systems as standard factory equipment across its entire line:

### Anti-lock Braking System (ABS) & Electronic Brakeforce Distribution (EBD)
On wet, slick asphalt or gravel patches, sudden braking can cause wheels to lock up, leading to a dangerous loss of steering control. The built-in ABS rapidly pumps the brakes automatically during sudden stops, allowing the driver to steer safely around hazards. Combined with EBD, the vehicle balances braking force dynamically across all four wheels based on payload distribution, keeping the vehicle stable even during sudden stops with a full passenger load.

### Driver Visibility and Awareness
Long hours behind the wheel require clear visibility to minimize fatigue. The Chery Wanda features an ergonomic driver's cabin equipped with a high-definition dashboard, a reverse visual camera system, and parking sensors. These tools give drivers an uncompromised view around the vehicle, making tight turnarounds at busy school gates or crowded tourist drop-offs safe and stress-free.

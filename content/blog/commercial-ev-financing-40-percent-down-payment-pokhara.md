## The 40/60 EV Financing Blueprint in Nepal

Under current Nepal Rastra Bank regulations, electric commercial vehicles enjoy highly favorable financing terms compared to their fossil-fuel counterparts. The breakdown is straightforward:

*   **Minimum Down Payment:** 40% of the vehicle’s total cost must be paid upfront by the buyer.
*   **Maximum Bank Financing:** Up to 60% of the vehicle’s cost can be covered through a commercial vehicle loan.

## Step-by-Step Commercial Loan Application Workflow

Navigating bank approvals can feel overwhelming, but our team at Trijal Motors acts as your direct intermediary to fast-track the process. Here is the exact path to ownership:

### 1. Vehicle Selection & Quotation
First, visit our Chauthe showroom to choose the ideal variant for your business—whether it’s an agile 11-seater for local school runs or a heavy-duty 16-seater high-roof model for long-distance tourist transit. We will issue an official corporate quotation based on your choice.

### 2. Gathering the Documentation Checklist
To apply for a commercial vehicle loan in Pokhara, you will need to compile the following official documents:
*   **Identification:** Original Citizenship Certificates of the promoters/owners.
*   **Business Registration:** Valid Company Registration, PAN/VAT certificates, and the firm's Article of Association if applicable.
*   **Financial Proof:** Audited financial statements or verified income sources, accompanied by 3 to 6 months of bank statements.

### 3. Bank Coordination & Approval
Trijal Motors maintains strong partnerships with leading commercial banks across Pokhara. We submit your quotation and document file directly to the credit department, guiding it through the appraisal process until the bank issues a Letter of Intent (LOI) or loan approval. This typically takes between 7 to 14 working days.

## Compliance, Route Permits, and Delivery

Once the bank approves the 60% financing, the remaining steps move quickly:

*   **Down Payment:** The buyer deposits the initial 40% down payment to Trijal Motors.
*   **Route Permits & Registration:** For commercial operations, a valid route permit must be registered with the local transport office. Our team assists you with the necessary paperwork to ensure full regulatory compliance.
*   **The Handover:** With the vehicle registered and the bank's disbursement complete, we host an official vehicle handover ceremony right here at our Pokhara-14 showroom.

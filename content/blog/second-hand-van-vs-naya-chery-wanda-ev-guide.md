## The True Cost of Used Diesel Assets: Hidden Liabilities

While a used diesel vehicle requires less initial capital upfront, it introduces immediate, unpredictable operating liabilities that can quickly drain your business cash flow:

### 1. Accelerating Mechanical Maintenance Costs
A second-hand diesel vehicle has already completed thousands of kilometers of heavy use. This means core mechanical components—including fuel injectors, turbochargers, clutch assemblies, and timing belts—are often nearing the end of their operational lifecycles, leading to high out-of-pocket repair costs.

### 2. Volatile Fuel Pump Expenses
Older internal combustion engines become less fuel-efficient over time. Spending high amounts of your daily fare revenue on expensive fossil fuels leaves your business with incredibly tight profit margins.

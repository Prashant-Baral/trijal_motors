## IP67 Ingress Protection: The Standard for Waterproofing

The core high-voltage components of the Chery Wanda—including the premium CATL Lithium Iron Phosphate battery packs, the permanent magnet synchronous electric motor, and the electronic control units—are completely sealed against environmental moisture.

*   **Understanding the IP67 Rating:** The "6" indicates complete protection against dust and particulate entry over long periods. The "7" means the high-voltage electrical enclosures are fully certified to withstand temporary immersion in water up to 1 meter deep without any liquid leakage or insulation breakdown.
*   **Heavy-Duty Battery Shielding:** To protect the battery packs from flying rocks, gravel, and rough under-car scrapes on unpaved resort access roads, the under-chassis is fitted with heavy-duty metal guard plates. This structural protection keeps the battery housing safe from puncture or distortion, as detailed in our [Chery Wanda safety features deep dive](/blog/chery-wanda-safety-features-nepal).

## Automated Multi-Layered Electrical Safeguards

Beyond physical seals and waterproofing, the vehicle's electrical system is continuously monitored by an intelligent **Battery Management System (BMS)**. This system acts as an automated safety monitor, providing constant protection for your fleet:

1.  **Continuous Leakage Monitoring:** The onboard computer continuously monitors insulation resistance values across all high-voltage lines. If the system detects even a slight electrical abnormality, it instantly alerts the driver via the digital dashboard display.
2.  **Automatic Crash and Emergency Cut-Off:** The vehicle is equipped with a smart sensor network. In the event of a significant impact, sudden deceleration, or unexpected electrical spike, the system automatically disconnects high-voltage power from the battery pack in milliseconds, completely eliminating any risk of shock.

## The Core Technical Difference

Understanding how each vehicle operates clarifies their long-term costs:

*   **Pure Electric Vehicles (EVs):** Driven entirely by a large battery pack and an electric motor. They use zero petrol, have no engine oil, produce zero emissions, and rely completely on affordable domestic electricity.
*   **Hybrid Vehicles:** Contain both a traditional petrol engine and a small helper battery. While they offer better mileage than standard gas cars, they still require regular petrol fill-ups, engine oil changes, and routine engine maintenance.

## Comparing the Financial Metrics

Let's look at how the long-term economics compare across three key areas:

| Cost Evaluation Metric | Pure Electric Vehicles (EVs) | Hybrid Vehicle Platforms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Government Import Taxes** | **Lowest Tax Bracket:** Benefits from highly supportive customs policies designed to promote green energy. | **Higher Tax Bracket:** Classified under traditional engine rules, leading to higher initial costs. |

For public transport route operators across Nepal, choosing between a traditional internal combustion vehicle and a new-energy commercial vehicle comes down to a clear economic question: *Does the math actually work out in the long run?*

Based on performance metrics gathered from active operators utilizing **Trijal Motors** commercial vehicles on major routes, here is the explicit cost breakdown proving why the **Chery Wanda EV microbus** thoroughly beats out diesel equivalents.

## Fuel and Energy Cost Comparison per Kilometer

With diesel fuel prices hovering around a steep Rs 230 per litre, maintaining a traditional internal combustion microbus on demanding mountain routes costs roughly Rs 8–9 per kilometer in fuel expenses alone. 

In sharp contrast, the **Chery Wanda** running on localized commercial electricity tariffs costs approximately **Rs 1.8 per kilometer** to charge. This translates to an immediate **70% to 80% drop in energy expenses**, drastically increasing your daily net take-home profit.

## Drastically Reduced Maintenance Overhead

Diesel powertrains depend on complex, high-friction systems that require frequent oil changes, fuel filters, timing belts, and exhaust repairs. 

The electric drivetrain inside the **Chery Wanda EV microbus** features far fewer moving parts. Furthermore, its advanced regenerative braking system automatically captures kinetic energy to recharge the battery while slowing down, reducing standard brake pad wear by more than half. ## Industrial-Grade CATL Battery Lifespan

Commercial reliability relies on the battery pack. The **Chery Wanda** utilizes a world-class CATL lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery, precisely scaled for maximum efficiency:
* **41.86 kWh** for the 11 and 12-seater variants.
* **53.58 kWh** for the heavy-duty 14 and 16-seater variants.

Unlike residential consumer electronics, LFP chemistry is customized specifically for deep, rigorous daily cycle profiles. ## The Break-Even and Long-Term Profit Picture

While the initial purchase price of a premium commercial **electric vehicle** can carry a premium over an older diesel counterpart, the extreme monthly operational savings alter the timeline rapidly. Most **Trijal Motors** clients completely recover the capital difference within the first 24 to 36 months of active operation. Past that break-even point, the **Chery Wanda** operates at an unmatched fraction of the cost, generating pure profit for the rest of its long service life.

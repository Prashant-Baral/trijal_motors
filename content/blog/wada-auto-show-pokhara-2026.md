**Trijal Motors** proudly participated in the landmark **WADA Auto Show**, held at the Pokhara Pradarshani Kendra. Organized by the Western Region Automobile Dealers Association (WADA) alongside the Nepal Automobile Dealers Association (NADA), the premier event served as the ultimate hub for automotive innovation in Gandaki Province.

## Complete Commercial EV Microbus Lineup on Display

As public transport rapidly shifts toward green energy, our stall drew massive crowds by showcasing the complete **Chery Wanda EV microbus** family. Fleet managers, route permit holders, and cooperative representatives from all over the region came to inspect the 11, 12, 14, and 16-seater variants up close.

The core attraction for visitors was the integration of high-performance CATL battery technology. Attendees were highly impressed by the real-world performance metrics of the **Chery Wanda** range, noting its rapid DC fast-charging capabilities as a game-changer for the **EV Pokhara** transit infrastructure.

## Exclusive Financing Schemes Launched

During the exhibition, the financial advisory desk at the **Trijal Motors** stall assisted dozens of operators with our competitive 40% down-payment financing schemes, built to make the transition into the commercial **electric vehicle (EV)** space as seamless and affordable as possible.

We extend our deepest gratitude to our customers, industry partners, and the transport associations who visited us at the WADA Auto Show. Stay tuned as we continue to lead the commercial **EV** evolution throughout Nepal!

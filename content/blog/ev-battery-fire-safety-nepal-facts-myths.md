## The Foundation of Safety: Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) Chemistry

The vast majority of modern electric vehicles running on our roads use **Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP)** battery packs rather than older chemistry types. LFP technology is selected by top manufacturers specifically because of its incredible structural safety:

*   **High Thermal Threshold:** LFP batteries have a very high thermal runaway temperature (exceeding 600°C), meaning they require extreme conditions to even begin overheating compared to standard electronics.
*   **Structural Durability:** The chemical bonds inside an LFP cell are structurally tough and highly stable, allowing them to handle intense heat, heavy daily usage, and fast-charging cycles without breaking down.

## Multi-Layered Protection Systems

Modern automotive battery packs do not rely on just one safety feature — they are built with multiple layers of automatic protection:

### Level 1: Intelligent Onboard BMS Monitoring

A dedicated Battery Management System continuously tracks the temperature and voltage of every single cell, instantly shutting off power if it detects any unusual patterns.

### Level 2: Advanced Liquid Cooling Systems

Active thermal management systems circulate specialized coolant through the battery pack, keeping temperatures perfectly stable whether climbing steep hills or fast-charging in summer heat.

### Level 3: Heavy Underbody Structural Armor

The entire battery pack is housed inside a reinforced high-strength steel cage, protecting the cells from loose gravel, rocks, and unexpected road impacts.

For travel agencies, tour operators, and destination management companies across Nepal, profitability depends entirely on one core corridor: the **Pokhara-Chitwan-Kathmandu Commercial Triangle**. This high-traffic tourism circuit moves thousands of international travelers every week between the heritage sites of the capital, the safari lodges of Sauraha, and the lakefront resorts of Pokhara.

Historically, keeping this vital transport loop moving meant dealing with high fuel expenditures and unpredictable diesel price spikes. Today, forward-thinking travel agencies are modernizing their businesses by shifting to clean, high-efficiency electric platforms.

As the premier commercial EV supplier in Gandaki Province, Trijal Motors Pvt. Ltd. (located at Pokhara-14, Chauthe) has mapped out the charging logistics and turnaround workflows needed to help your travel agency maximize its returns across this profitable transit triangle.

## Mapping the Logistics: Charging Hub Positioning

The Chery Wanda 14-seater and 16-seater high-roof variants feature an advanced **53.58 kWh CATL battery pack**, engineered to deliver reliable performance across long highway routes. By utilizing the standardized GBT charging protocol, your drivers can access high-output public DC fast chargers across all major highways. Here is how leading operators manage each leg of the triangle:

### 1. The Pokhara to Kathmandu Leg (Prithvi Highway — ~200 km)
*   **The Workflow:** Vehicles depart with a 100% overnight charge from your primary depot or our showroom terminal.
*   **The Charging Stop:** Drivers make a scheduled 30-minute stop at the busy Mugling or Kurintar charging cluster. While your passengers enjoy a lunch break, the vehicle connects to a high-output DC fast charger, quickly boosting the battery from 30% to 80% to comfortably handle the steep climb up the Thankot incline. For more details on highway charging, refer to our [Pokhara-to-Kathmandu highway range and logistics guide](/blog/pokhara-to-kathmandu-electric-microbus-highway-guide).

### 2. The Kathmandu to Chitwan Leg (Mugling-Narayangarh Corridor — ~150 km)
*   **The Workflow:** Vehicles depart from Kathmandu valley and descend smoothly toward the plains.
*   **The Charging Stop:** The continuous downhill descent allows the regenerative braking system to automatically capture energy, feeding power back into the battery cells. A quick, simple top-up at Narayangarh ensures your vehicle arrives at your Sauraha resort destination with a healthy energy buffer.

### 3. The Chitwan to Pokhara Leg (Siddhartha/Prithvi Link — ~150 km)
*   **The Workflow:** Vehicles travel from the flat terrains of Chitwan back up into the hills of Kaski.
*   **The Charging Stop:** Utilizing high-output charging infrastructure installations along the route allows your drivers to easily manage the trip, ensuring your passengers enjoy a smooth, quiet, and exceptionally comfortable ride.

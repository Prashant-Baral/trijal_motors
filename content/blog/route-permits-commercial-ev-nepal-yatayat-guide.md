## Step-by-Step Guide to Securing EV Route Permits

The Government of Nepal offers supportive regulatory pathways designed to encourage transport operators to adopt sustainable electric vehicles:

1.  **Cooperative Recommendation Letter:** Your first step is to get an official support letter from your transport cooperative confirming your vehicle's placement on your chosen route.
2.  **Department of Transport Management (DoTM) Filing:** Submit your vehicle's technical specifications—including its high-strength steel safety structure and CATL battery certifications—to the local transport office for review.
3.  **Green-Plate Commercial Allocation:** Once approved, your vehicle receives its official green commercial license plates, giving your company full authorization to collect passenger fares across your designated route.

## Step-by-Step Logistics Map: The Pokhara-to-Kathmandu Corridor

Managing a successful intercity commercial EV route comes down to treating your charging stops as valuable rest breaks for your passengers. Here is how leading tourism operators structure the 200 km Prithvi Highway run using the Chery Wanda platform:

### 1. The Departure: Starting Fresh from Pokhara
Your journey begins with an overnight AC charge at your main garage or at our Chauthe showroom terminal. Charging slowly overnight balances individual battery cells perfectly and utilizes lower off-peak industrial electricity tariffs. The vehicle departs with a 100% State of Charge (SoC).

### 2. The Mid-Way Break: Mugling and Kurintar (~90 km out)
As the vehicle travels through Damauli and descends toward Dumre, the regenerative braking system automatically captures energy on long downhill stretches, feeding power back into the battery cells. By the time you reach the busy Mugling or Kurintar transit area, the battery will generally sit between 40% and 50% SoC depending on your driving style and payload.

This is the perfect time for a scheduled stop. While passengers take a lunch break, the driver plugs the vehicle into a high-output public **GBT DC Fast Charger**. In just 30 to 45 minutes, the battery quickly tops up to 80%, giving you plenty of energy buffer to comfortably handle the steep climb up the Thankot incline into the Kathmandu valley.

### 3. The Arrival: Destination Turnaround
Upon reaching Kathmandu, the vehicle is offloaded. While preparing for the return leg, the vehicle can be connected to any local urban fast-charging hub at Kalanki, Balkhu, or central bus terminals, ensuring your fleet is quickly ready to head back to Pokhara.

## Implementing Smart Highway Driving Habits

To get the absolute best range out of your Chery Wanda fleet, drivers should practice three essential highway habits:

1.  **Maintain Steady Cruising Speeds:** Electric motors are incredibly efficient, but driving at excessive speeds increases aerodynamic drag and drains energy quickly. Maintaining a steady, smooth speed maximizes your range.
2.  **Master Regenerative Braking:** Teach drivers to avoid sudden, aggressive braking. By lifting off the accelerator early and letting the motor naturally slow the vehicle down on highway descents, they can maximize energy recovery.
3.  **Monitor Your Charging Limits:** For highway fast-charging stops, it is most efficient to unplug once the battery hits 80% SoC. The charging speed naturally slows down after 80% to protect the battery cells. Unplugging at 80% saves valuable time and keeps your trip moving efficiently.

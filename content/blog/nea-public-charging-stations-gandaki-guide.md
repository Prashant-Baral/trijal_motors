## Core Charging Hubs Across Gandaki Province

The Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) has strategically placed high-power **DC Fast Chargers** along major highways and transport corridors to ensure smooth, continuous travel:

*   **Pokhara Charging Hub:** Located near the main tourist and transport sectors, providing high-speed dual-gun DC chargers perfect for quick top-ups while running errands or waiting for passengers.
*   **Damauli & Dumre Stations:** Perfectly positioned along the Prithvi Highway, allowing drivers traveling between Pokhara and Kathmandu to recharge smoothly during a quick 20-minute tea break.
*   **Waling & Syangja Points:** Crucial charging locations along the Siddharth Highway, supporting seamless travel down toward Palpa and Bhairahawa.

## Straightforward Charging Costs and Easy Digital Payments

Using the public charging network is highly affordable and fully digital, keeping your operational costs transparent:

### 1. Low Operational Charging Cost

Charging rates vary slightly by time of day, but average roughly Rs. 8 to Rs. 12 per unit (kWh). A quick 30-minute fast charge costs only a few hundred rupees — far less than a single petrol fill-up. For a month-by-month electricity cost model, read our [petrol vs. EV running cost comparison](/blog/petrol-vs-ev-running-costs-nepal).

### 2. The Mobile Application System

Drivers use the official NEA charging mobile application to locate active stations, scan QR codes on the charger, and monitor real-time energy flow directly from their phone.

### 3. Seamless Digital Wallets

You can easily top up your charging balance using familiar local digital wallets like eSewa or Khalti, making payments quick and simple without needing cash.
